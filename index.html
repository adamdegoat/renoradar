📖 Renovation Terms Explained
DB Box
Distribution Board. The electrical panel that distributes power to different circuits in your home. Upgrading it adds more circuit capacity.
PSF / Per SqFt
Price per square foot. Common unit for tiling, flooring, painting and hacking. Multiply by your room area to get total cost.
Linear Ft
Measurement along a straight line. Used for carpentry (wardrobes, kitchen cabinets), countertops and false ceiling runs.
R32 / R410A
Types of refrigerant gas used in air conditioning systems. R32 is newer and more eco-friendly. Top-up is only needed if there is a leak.
Rectified Tiles
Tiles cut to precise dimensions with sharp edges. Required for large-format tiles and minimal grout lines.
Waterproofing
A membrane layer applied under bathroom tiles to prevent water seepage. Critical — never skip it.
Fancoil
The indoor unit of a split air conditioning system — the part mounted on your wall or ceiling.
Chemical Overhaul
A deep cleaning of the aircon evaporator coil using chemical solution. Only needed every 2-3 years, not every service.
MR Board
Moisture-Resistant board. Used in wet areas like kitchen and bathrooms. Budget contractors often substitute standard particleboard.
Hacking
Demolition work — removing existing tiles, walls, or structures before new installations. Priced per sqft.
L-Box
The recessed cove in a false ceiling that holds LED strip lighting. Creates an indirect lighting effect.
ID Firm
Interior Design firm. Full-service renovation companies that design and manage the entire project. Premium pricing but full coordination.
❓ Frequently Asked Questions
How often is this data updated?
The RenoRadar intelligence engine scrapes renovation forums three times a week (Monday, Wednesday, Friday) and updates price benchmarks every Wednesday night. The L9 AI engine also discovers new overcharge patterns weekly.
Are these prices inclusive of GST?
Most renovation contractor quotes in Singapore are GST-exclusive. Add 9% on top of all prices shown here when comparing against your contractor's final invoice.
Why are there three price tiers?
Budget tier reflects direct sub-contractors with no project management overhead. Mid-range covers small renovation firms with some design support. Premium covers full-service ID firms that manage the entire project — you pay for their coordination, not just the work.
My quote is higher than the Premium tier — is that normal?
Not necessarily. Very high quotes can reflect: (1) condo surcharges for management restrictions, (2) unusual scope like structural work, (3) premium material choices, or (4) contractor padding. Use the overcharge patterns above to investigate.
Should I always go for the cheapest contractor?
No. Budget tier contractors are legitimate, but they require more active supervision. Payment terms, material specifications and timeline commitments must all be documented in writing regardless of which tier you choose.
How do I use this page alongside my actual quote?
Open your contractor quote on one screen and RenoRadar on another. Go through your quote line by line. For each item, find the matching category and item here, check which tier your quote falls into, and look at the overcharge risk rating.
